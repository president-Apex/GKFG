## WHY INDIVIDUAL STATE PAGES?

Three strategic reasons:

1. SEO: “LLC formation Kansas City Missouri” and “LLC formation Overland Park Kansas”
   are different searches. One page cannot rank for both. Individual state + city pages
   are how you capture local search intent in all 7 states.
1. Trust: A Charlotte entrepreneur landing on a page that says “Serving Charlotte,
   Concord, and Huntersville” trusts GKFG more than one that says “Serving 18+ cities.”
   Specificity = credibility.
1. Compliance: Each state has different filing fees, rules, and notary laws.
   State-specific pages let you surface that information without cluttering the main site.

Always confirm with your receiving party that they accept electronically notarized documents.

Q: How long does Georgia LLC formation take?
A: Georgia processes online filings in 7–10 business days — longer than most states. Plan accordingly, especially if you need your LLC active before signing a contract or opening a bank account.

Q: What is Georgia’s annual registration fee?
A: Georgia LLCs must file an annual registration by April 1 each year and pay a $50 fee. Missing this can result in administrative dissolution.
