Color palette

Primary colors

Use these:
	•	Navy Blue — main brand color
	•	Charcoal / Deep Gray — text, background accents
	•	White — clean space and clarity
	•	Muted Gold — premium accent color

Why

This gives:
	•	trust
	•	professionalism
	•	seriousness
	•	premium feel

Exact visual direction
	•	Navy: deep, rich, not bright royal blue
	•	Gold: subtle, not shiny yellow
	•	Gray: clean and modern
	•	White: lots of it for breathing room

Avoid
	•	bright red
	•	neon blue
	•	bright green
	•	orange-heavy palettes
	•	anything that feels like a bail bonds flyer
