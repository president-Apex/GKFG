Include on all service pages and intake forms

	•	USPTO trademark fees are government fees paid directly to the United States Patent and Trademark Office. Current base fee is $350 per class of goods or services as of January 18, 2025.
	•	GKFG provides document preparation and filing services. GKFG is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. For legal advice specific to your situation, consult a licensed attorney.
	•	Registered agent service is available in Missouri only. GKFG does not provide registered agent service in other states.
	•	RON (Remote Online Notarization) services are available in the 20 states where Missouri notaries are authorized to serve remote signers.
	•	Annual report fees and filing deadlines vary by state and entity type. Clients are responsible for maintaining compliance in their state of registration. GKFG offers compliance monitoring as an add-on service.
