## YOUR INSTINCT IS RIGHT — LEAD WITH VALUE, NOT MONEY

The partner program is built on three things:

1. Warm client introductions (actual revenue for them)
1. Visibility and credibility (logo on GKFG site, featured listing)
1. Collaborative growth (co-branded events, joint content)

NO referral fees. NO cash commissions. NO revenue sharing.
Reason: CPAs, attorneys, and licensed bankers have ethics rules around referral compensation.
Leading with fees can end a conversation before it starts.
Leading with “I’ll send you clients” opens every door.

A: Yes — rush fees apply for appointments booked with less than 4 hours notice. All rush fees are listed above.
